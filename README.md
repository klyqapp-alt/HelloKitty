# Hello Kitty's Love Adventure 💕

A cute catching game made with love! Help Hello Kitty catch falling hearts, bows, and cupcakes.

## How to Play
- Use ← → arrow keys or touch/mouse to move
- Catch hearts 💕, bows 🎀, cupcakes 🧁, and more!
- Avoid rain clouds ☁️ and broken hearts 💔
- Special items like rainbows 🌈 and stars ⭐ give bonus points!

## Play Online
Open `index.html` in your browser to play!
